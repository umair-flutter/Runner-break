Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Runebreaker (package com.umairkhalid.runebreaker), published by Umair Khalid ("we", "us"), handles information. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location, and we do not require an account.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to show personalised or non-personalised ads, limit how often you see an ad, and detect fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and where else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Ad Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Players can remove banner and interstitial ads with the "No Ads" purchase. Rewarded ads are optional and only play when you choose to watch one.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to deliver your item.

4. Notifications and sharing

Reminder notifications (e.g. "your free spin is ready") are created on your device; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android settings. If you tap Share, the game creates a screenshot and opens Android's share menu; nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

5. Children

Runebreaker is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it (Settings > Delete All Progress) or uninstall the game. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policies above. To ask a question or make a deletion request, email us at the address below.

7. Security

Save data is encrypted and signed on your device. Network traffic from the ad and billing SDKs uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, most requests are handled by deleting your local data or through Google's controls. Contact us for any request.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
